		index of "any" single in single-cards.txt of "LEGAL SINGLE NAME".

# face-index.dat
		1-Serial#
		2-Parent#
		3-layout  (0=normal, 1= Flip, 2=Split)
		4-Edition# (zero based)
		5-NameIndex
		6-ManacostIndex
		7-TypelineIndex
		8-Has PT
		 -Power# (only present if has PT)
		 -Tough# (only present if has PT)
		9-NumText#
 		 - (if NumText>0) TextIndex (zero or more)

Note: NameIndex, ManacostIndex, TypelineIndex and TextIndex are 1-based indexes. (subtract 1 when using)
